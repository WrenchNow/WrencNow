Mobile mechanic services

Car maintenance and repair at your home, office or wherever your car is parked. Background-checked mobile mechanics near you send upfront quotes, and you pay after the job.

  • Mobile oil changeGet your oil changed in your driveway or office parking lot.
  • Mobile brake repairSquealing or grinding brakes? Mobile mechanics replace brake pads, rotors and calipers at your home or work.
  • Car battery replacementCar won't start? A mobile mechanic tests your battery, starter and alternator, and replaces the battery where your car is parked.
  • Mobile car diagnosticCheck engine light on, or a noise you can't place? A mobile mechanic diagnoses your car at home.
  • Starter and alternator replacementCar won't crank or the battery keeps dying? Mobile mechanics test and replace starters and alternators at your home or work.
  • Pre-purchase car inspectionBuying a used car? A mobile mechanic meets you at the seller and inspects it before you pay.
  • Cooling system repairOverheating or leaking coolant? Mobile mechanics repair radiators, hoses, thermostats and water pumps at your home.
  • Suspension and steering repairClunks over bumps or a bouncy ride? Mobile mechanics replace shocks, struts, control arms, tie rods and sway bar links at your home.
  • Car AC and heater repairAC blowing warm or heater not working? Mobile mechanics diagnose and repair car air conditioning and heat at your home or work.
  • Tire rotation and tire serviceGet your tires rotated, swapped or checked at home.
  • Serpentine belt replacementSquealing belt or cracked serpentine belt? Mobile mechanics replace belts and tensioners at your home or work.
  • Tune-up and spark plugsRough idle or misfires? Mobile mechanics replace spark plugs, ignition coils and filters at your home.

Why use a mobile mechanic?

A mobile mechanic brings the shop to you. There's no drive to a garage, no waiting room and no ride to arrange. Because independent mobile mechanics don't pay shop rent, their prices are often well below a dealer's, and every WrenchNow quote shows the dealer estimate beside it.

Mobile auto repair covers most maintenance and many repairs. Jobs that need a lift or heavy equipment, like transmission rebuilds or wheel alignment, still need a shop, and your mechanic will tell you before quoting.
