Used car inspection, at the seller's driveway

Before you hand over money for a used car, have a mechanic look at it. A mobile mechanic meets you wherever the car is, at a private seller's house or a dealer lot, and tells you what you are really buying.

Get pre-purchase car inspection quotesHow it works

Signs you need it

  • You are buying from a private seller
  • The price seems too good to be true
  • The car has high miles or no service records
  • You want a list of repairs to negotiate the price down

What the mobile mechanic does on site

  • Scans for stored and pending trouble codes
  • Checks the engine, transmission, brakes, suspension, tires and fluids
  • Looks for leaks, rust, accident repairs and warning signs
  • Test-drives the car and gives you a plain summary of what needs work

Good to know: Ask the seller for a time when the engine is cold. Some problems only show on a cold start.

How booking works

Describe the job and your car, and background-checked mobile mechanics near you send upfront quotes with labor, parts and a time. Each quote shows an estimate of the dealer price next to it. Pick one, and the mechanic comes to your home or work. Your card is charged after the job, and any change in price needs your approval in the app first.
