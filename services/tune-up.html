Tune-up and spark plugs, in your driveway

Worn spark plugs and coils cause rough running, misfires and poor fuel economy. A mobile mechanic can replace them, along with air and cabin filters, without you leaving home.

Get tune-up and spark plugs quotesHow it works

Signs you need it

  • Rough idle, shaking or hesitation when accelerating
  • A misfire code or flashing check engine light
  • Hard starting
  • Your car has reached the spark plug interval in its manual

What the mobile mechanic does on site

  • Replaces spark plugs to the correct gap and torque
  • Tests and replaces failing ignition coils
  • Replaces engine air and cabin air filters
  • Clears codes and test-drives the car

Good to know: A flashing check engine light usually means a misfire that can damage the catalytic converter. Drive gently and book soon.
