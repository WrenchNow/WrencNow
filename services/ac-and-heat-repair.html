Car AC and heater repair, wherever you are

When the AC blows warm or the heater blows cold, a mobile mechanic can find out why and fix many causes on the spot, from a refrigerant leak to a blower motor.

Get car ac and heater repair quotesHow it works

Signs you need it

  • The AC blows warm or only works sometimes
  • The heater blows cold, or the windows won't defog
  • The fan only works on some speeds
  • A musty smell from the vents

What the mobile mechanic does on site

  • Checks AC pressures and looks for leaks
  • Recharges refrigerant when the system is sealed
  • Replaces blower motors, resistors and cabin air filters
  • Diagnoses heater problems, which are often in the cooling system

Good to know: Topping up refrigerant without fixing a leak only buys a few weeks. A good mechanic finds the leak first.
