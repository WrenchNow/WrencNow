# First 30 days of posting: plan (2026-10-21)

Built for someone working restaurant shifts: **one filming block a week, plus about 10 minutes a day.**

## Before day 1 (must be true first)
- [ ] Scanning works on your phone (Worker deployed with the free allowance, or your own key). Scripts 2 and 5 depend on it.
- [ ] The app shows "allergy flags are a helper, not a guarantee." Script 6 and Post 6 say it, so the app should too. Right now `src/app.js` doesn't have this copy.
- [ ] Amazon: Associates tag set and staples ASINs verified **before** anything shows the cart (Post 7, Launch Post A). Every Amazon mention carries the disclosure.
- [ ] Accounts: TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest (business account) and X or Threads. Use the same handle everywhere. Put the link in your bio (it says "coming soon" until launch).
- [ ] The 31.9% source (Yu & Jaenicke 2020) is checked and saved to paste into comments.

## Weekly rhythm
| When | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Day off (1 block) | Film 2–3 videos and cook the pin dishes (shoot the photos while you're at it). Screen-record the app the same day. | 2–3 hrs |
| Same day | Schedule the week's pins (Pinterest's built-in scheduler) and posts (Meta Business Suite for IG). | 30 min |
| Before or after a shift | Post the day's video or post and reply to comments. | 10 min |

**Cadence:** 3 short videos a week (same video on TikTok, Reels and Shorts), 1 pin a day (schedule all 7 at once), 3 text posts a week on X/Threads. If a week gets slammed, keep the pins going (they're scheduled) and drop to 2 videos. Don't skip whole weeks.

## What to film first (in this order)
1. **Script 4, Sad spinach rescue.** Cheapest to make, works without the scan feature, and it's a real recipe.
2. **Script 1, A third of your groceries.** Your stat hook. Only needs a receipt and a trash can.
3. **Script 3, Restaurant server built this.** Film it in your work clothes right after a shift. Real beats polished.
4. **Script 2 (fridge) and Script 5 (receipt).** Only after scanning works on your phone.
5. **Script 6, Allergies.** Only after the disclaimer is in the app.

## The 30 days
- **Week 1:** Scripts 4 and 1 plus Post 3. Start pins 1–7. Goal: get into the habit of posting, not get views.
- **Week 2:** Script 3 plus Posts 1, 2 and 4. Pins 8–10, then re-pin your best one with a new image.
- **Week 3:** Scripts 2 and 5 (if scanning works) plus Posts 5, 8 and 9. Re-film your best hook from weeks 1–2 with a new opening line.
- **Week 4:** Script 6 plus Posts 6 and 10. If the app is live, run the launch sequence (Posts A, B and C) and pause the "coming soon" posts.

## What to measure (once a week, 15 minutes, one spreadsheet)
- **Videos:** % who watched past 3 seconds (tells you if the hook works), average watch time, shares, saves.
- **Pins:** saves and outbound clicks. Impressions matter less.
- **Posts:** replies and link clicks.
- **Bio link:** clicks per week. Use a separate tracked link per platform so you can see where people come from.
- **After launch:** people who open the app, finish onboarding and scan once. Track clicks to Amazon in the Associates dashboard, not guesses.

**Rule of thumb at day 30:** double down on the 2 hooks with the best 3-second hold, cut whatever got no saves or shares, and write batch 2 around what worked. Don't judge anything off a single post.
