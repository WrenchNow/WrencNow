# Ad creatives: 12 concepts for Instagram and Facebook (2026-10-22)

Four concepts per stage of the audience ladder (see `2026-10-22-paid-social-plan.md`). Every one can be filmed on a phone in a home kitchen, in one afternoon off.

**Rules built into every concept:**
- **Every ad links to the Pantry Pal landing page.** Never to Amazon. **No ad says "Amazon" or shows its logo**, so the ads themselves don't need the Associates disclosure. The landing page carries it.
- **No ad mentions allergies.** Diet filtering appears in 2C, and the allergy helper note lives on the landing page.
- **No ad tells viewers what they are** ("broke", "student", "parent", "over 50"). Lines are first person or about the food.
- **No testimonials.** People on camera only do and say what really happened. Lines marked **[only if true]** get cut if they aren't.
- **Only built features are shown.** Fridge and receipt scans need the Worker live (section 0 of the plan). Film those after scanning works on a fresh phone.
- **The 31.9% stat** is Yu & Jaenicke (2020), *American Journal of Agricultural Economics*. Its source is on the landing page. Don't turn it into a dollar amount.
- **Character limits:** primary text is ≤125 characters and headlines are ≤40 (counts in brackets). Meta cuts off text after about 125 characters in most placements.
- **CTA button:** "Learn more" for everything. It's honest for a landing page and it's on Meta's standard list. Test "Sign up" later only if accounts exist.
- **Film format:** vertical 9:16 for Reels; 1:1 or 4:5 for carousel and static. Keep text in the middle 70% of the frame so buttons and captions don't cover it. Add captions to every video (most people watch muted).

## Stage 1: ages 18–28

### 1A. The receipt rip
- **Format:** Reel 9:16, about 15s
- **Target:** Stage 1 (also try it for Stage 2)
- **1-second hook:** hands rip the bottom third off a long grocery receipt. On-screen text: **"This part of your groceries? Trash."**

| Time | Shot | Voiceover | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1s | Close-up: the receipt rip. | (rip sound) | This part of your groceries? Trash. |
| 1–4s | Drop the strip in the trash. Lid slams. | "That's roughly how much food US households throw out." | US households waste ~31.9% of the food they buy |
| 4–8s | Open your fridge: wilting spinach, half an onion, a lonely lemon. | "It's the 'I'll use it tomorrow' stuff." | The "I'll use it tomorrow" stuff |
| 8–12s | Screen recording: Pantry tab, the "Use within 3 days" number, then tap "Cook with what's expiring." | "This shows what's about to go bad and what to cook with it." | What's expiring → what to cook |
| 12–15s | Tape the strip back on the receipt. Thumbs up. | "Use it first. Buy less." | Pantry Pal. Free. |

- **Primary text [104]:** US households waste about 31.9% of the food they buy. This app shows what to cook before yours goes bad.
- **Headline [25]:** Use it before you lose it
- **CTA:** Learn more

## Stage 2: ages 25–35

- **Format:** Reel 9:16, about 20s

| Time | Shot | Line | On-screen text |

### 2D. Paste the order
- **Format:** Static image, 4:5. Left: a phone showing a generic grocery order email (blur any store logo). Right: the Pantry Pal pantry list filled in. Arrow between them.
- **Target:** Stage 2
- **1-second hook:** big overlay text across the top: **"Groceries came. Typing it all in? No."**

**Image text:**
- Top: Groceries came. Typing it all in? No.
- Under the left phone: 1. Paste the order email
- Under the right phone: 2. Check the list. Done.
- Bottom strip: Pantry Pal · free

**How to make it:** screen-record Scan → "Paste order" with a real order of yours and take two screenshots (the paste box and the review list). Blur your name, address and order number. Blur or crop the store logo.

**Accuracy note:** Paste order reads order text from Amazon, Instacart and Walmart emails or order pages. You then review the list and add it. It doesn't happen automatically, so the ad says "check the list."

- **Primary text [85]:** Groceries came? Paste the order email, check the list, done. No typing in every item.
- **Headline [31]:** Paste the order. Pantry's done.
- **CTA:** Learn more

## Stage 3: ages 40–60

**Facebook style (3A, 3B, 3C):** slower pace, bigger text (aim for 2–3 words per line on screen), warm natural light, real food, and one benefit per ad. No slang, no fast cuts, no meme sounds. Show hands and food more than faces. If a person is on camera, use a real adult home cook (a creator or a family member who agrees), and have them **do** things, not give opinions about the app.

### 3A. "What can I make with what I have?" (Facebook style)
- **Format:** Reel 9:16, about 25s, calm pace. Also export a 4:5 version for the Facebook Feed.
- **Target:** Stage 3
- **1-second hook:** a full fridge door swings open. Large on-screen text: **"What can I make with what I have?"**

| Time | Shot | Voiceover (calm) | On-screen text (large) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Fridge door opens, full shelves. | "What can I make with what I have?" | What can I make with what I have? |
| 2–7s | Hands hold the phone, Scan tab, "Fridge," one photo. Slow and clear. | "Take one photo of the fridge." | Step 1: One photo |
| 7–12s | Screen recording, slowed down: the list of items found. | "It lists the food it sees. You can fix anything." | Step 2: Check the list |
| 12–18s | Screen recording: Cook tab, "Ready now." Scroll slowly and stop on one recipe. | "Then it shows recipes you can make tonight, with what's already there." | Step 3: Cook tonight |
| 18–25s | Hands plating a simple dinner (e.g. Pasta al Burro, 15 min in the app). | "Free to use. Nothing to install." | Free · Works in your phone's browser |

- **Primary text [97]:** Snap a photo of your fridge and see what you can make tonight. 232 recipes built in. Free to use.
- **Headline [33]:** What can I make with what I have?
- **CTA:** Learn more

### 3B. Three brown bananas (Facebook style)
- **Format:** Static image, 1:1 (also crop to 4:5). Natural light, wooden or tile counter.
- **Target:** Stage 3
- **1-second hook:** the image itself: **three very brown bananas on the left, a loaf of banana bread on the right**, with large text **"Not trash. Banana bread."**

**Image text:**
- Top (large): Not trash. Banana bread.
- Small, bottom left: Pantry Pal shows what's about to go bad, and what to make with it.
- Small, bottom right: Free

**How to make it:** buy bananas, wait a week (honestly the hardest part), bake the app's Banana Bread recipe (it uses 3 bananas), and shoot both in the same spot. Real photo only. No stock images, no AI images.

- **Primary text [113]:** Three brown bananas = banana bread, not trash. Pantry Pal tracks what's about to go bad and what to make with it.
- **Headline [28]:** Use it up before it goes bad
- **CTA:** Learn more

### 3C. A week of dinners, pantry first (Facebook style)
- **Format:** Carousel, 4 cards, 1:1. Each card = a real photo of a dinner + one big line. Last card = a simple app screenshot.
- **Target:** Stage 3
- **1-second hook (card 1):** a calendar on the fridge with "Mon, Tue, Wed…" handwritten, and text **"This week's dinners: planned."**

| Card | Image | Overlay text (large) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A handwritten week calendar on the fridge | This week's dinners: planned. |
| 2 | A real dinner photo (e.g. White Chicken Chili) | Uses what's in your kitchen first |

| 4 | "Plan my week" screenshot | One list for what's missing. Free. |

**Note:** Dinners only, as in 2B. Pick recipes that fit the household in the screenshot. Crop out any Amazon buttons.

- **Primary text [108]:** Sunday planning, done: a week of dinners that uses what's in your kitchen first, plus one list for the rest.
- **Headline [31]:** A week of dinners, pantry first
- **CTA:** Learn more

### 3D. Send this to your mom (the kid → parent bridge)
- **Format:** Reel 9:16, about 20s. Runs on **Instagram** (where the kids are) aimed at Stage 1 and 2 people, but the content is for their parents.
- **Target:** Stage 3, reached through Stages 1 and 2
- **1-second hook:** a fridge door full of condiment jars. On-screen text: **"Every mom's fridge door:"**

> **Only film this with a real parent who agrees to be on camera.** Wyatt with his own mom or dad, or a creator with theirs. The parent's lines are reactions in the moment, not opinions about the app. If no parent is available, film it with the hands-only version (no face, no parent lines).

| Time | Shot | Line | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1s | Fridge door: three open jars of the same mustard. | — | Every mom's fridge door: |
| 1–5s | You hold the phone up to the parent's fridge and snap it. | You: "Okay Mom, hold still, I'm scanning your fridge." | Scanning Mom's fridge |
| 5–10s | Screen recording: the list of items. Zoom in on "mustard (3)". | Parent: [real reaction only] | 3 mustards. Three. |
| 10–15s | Screen recording: "What's for dinner tonight?" picks. | You: "Look, it picks dinner from what you've already got." | Dinner from what's already there |
| 15–20s | Both of you at the counter. You point at the camera, then down toward the share button. | You: "Send this to your mom." | Send this to your mom |

- **Primary text [107]:** Send this to whoever has three open jars of mustard. It keeps track of the fridge and what to cook from it.
- **Headline [21]:** Send this to your mom
- **CTA:** Learn more
