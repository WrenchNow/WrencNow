# Video scripts, batch 1 (2026-10-21)

Six short scripts for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Everything is filmable on a phone in a home kitchen. Film vertical (9:16) and keep on-screen text in the middle 70% of the frame so app buttons don't cover it.

**Before you post:**
- The app isn't public yet. Swap `[APP LINK]` for the real link at launch. Until then, end with "Follow so you see it when it's out" instead of the link line.
- Any script that shows or mentions the Amazon cart has the disclosure written into the caption. Keep it there.
- Fridge and receipt scanning need the AI connection turned on (the free allowance on the Worker or your own key). Make sure it works on your phone before you film Scripts 2 and 5.
- The "one cart" shot only works for items that have a verified ASIN in the staples catalog. Without one, the item opens as an Amazon search. Film the cart shot after the ASINs are filled in, or show the shopping list instead.

**Source for the stat:** "US households waste about 31.9% of the food they buy" is from the brief. It's usually credited to Yu & Jaenicke (2020), *American Journal of Agricultural Economics*. Wyatt, check that before you pin it in a comment. Don't turn it into a dollar figure. We don't have a sourced dollar amount for an average household.
