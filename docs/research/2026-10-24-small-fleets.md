# Small fleets: what they need from WrenchNow (Oct 24, 2026)

## Findings
- **Most small fleets are run by one person.** In a Nov 2025 survey of 2,000+ small fleet operators (92% under 50
  vehicles), 65% run fleet operations alone and another 26% have only 2–3 people. About 80% spend 10–30+ hours a week
  on fleet tasks.
  [Work Truck](https://www.worktruckonline.com/10250402/vms-survey-finds-65-of-small-fleet-managers-run-operations-alone),
  [AutoSuccess](https://www.autosuccessonline.com/vms-small-fleet-management-survey-results/)
- **The biggest time sinks are paperwork/manual records (28.5%) and scheduling repairs (23%).** 54% have frequent
  trouble scheduling repairs, and 52.6% say unplanned breakdowns regularly disrupt operations. (Same sources.)
- **Downtime is expensive.** 41% of fleets under 50 vehicles estimate $10K–$50K a year in downtime losses, and 14.5%
  say over $100K. (Same sources.)
- **Caveat:** the survey was published by a fleet-software vendor (VMS), the answers are self-reported, and the sample
  is online. Treat the numbers as directional. *(verify before using them in marketing)*
- **Fleets want consolidated, standardized records:** batch invoicing, cost centers, and reporting across all vehicles.
  [beancount.io guide](https://beancount.io/ko/blog/2026/05/28/mobile-mechanic-on-site-repair-business-bookkeeping-premium-billing-actual-expense-vs-standard-mileage-section-179-scan-tools-parts-markup-matrix-comeback-warranty-reserves-ase-certification-guide)
- **Older data:** a 2014 reader survey found that small fleets run vehicles to high mileage, so maintenance is a top
  issue. [Business Fleet](https://www.businessfleet.com/blogposts/what-keeps-small-fleets-up-at-night)

## What this means for the MVP
1. **Keep it to one login.** The owner or manager books for every vehicle. Team seats can come later.
2. **Kill the paperwork.** Jobs are tagged to the business, and there's a monthly statement per vehicle that can be
   printed or saved as PDF with totals. A billing email is stored for receipts.
3. **Scheduling is already ours.** On-site service means no drop-off and no driver downtime. Use that in fleet copy,
   but don't use the survey dollar figures as our own claims.
4. **Not in the MVP:** net-30 invoicing (all payments still go through card holds, per the business rules), multiple
   users, cost centers, and fleet discounts. These are listed as questions for the owner.
