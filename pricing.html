Pricing, in full

What drivers pay, what mechanics pay, and where the money goes.

Drivers

  • Requesting and comparing quotesFree
  • The jobThe price on the quote you accept: labor plus partsQuoted price
  • Booking fee$0
  • Diagnosis visitFree or a set fee, shown on the mechanic's profile and quoteVaries
  • Price changesOnly if you approve them in the appYour call

Your card is charged after the job. Quotes include an estimate of what a typical dealer would charge for the same work.

Mechanics

  • JoiningFree for founding mechanics$0
  • Platform fee per job5%
  • Card processingStripe's card and payout costs3.2% + 55¢
  • OnboardingFrom your first paid jobs, up to $10 each, max 15% of a job$50 total
  • Featured placementOptional$29/mo

When the founding offer ends, new mechanics pay a one-time $49 verification fee instead of the onboarding fee.

Where $200 goes

Most of every job goes to the mechanic who did the work. WrenchNow keeps 5%, and card processing covers what Stripe charges to move the money.

No surprises on either side.
