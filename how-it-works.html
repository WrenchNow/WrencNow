How WrenchNow works

From "my car is making a noise" to paid and done, without leaving home.

For drivers

  1. Request quotes

    Describe the job, add your car and up to three photos, and choose up to three days and times of day. Send it to mechanics you pick, or to everyone nearby who does that work. Requests stay open for three days.

  2. Compare and accept

    Quotes arrive in My bookings, cheapest first, each with labor, parts, a day and time, and an estimate of what a dealer would charge. Accept one and Stripe saves your card. Nothing is charged yet.

  3. Job done, then pay

    The mechanic comes to you. If more work is needed, they send a revised price that you approve or reject. After the job, confirm and pay, or let it charge automatically after 48 hours.

When something goes wrong

A problem with the work. Press Report a problem before you're charged. The payment is held while we talk to you both.

The mechanic didn't come. Report the no-show after the appointment time. You pay nothing, and it's recorded on their profile.

Asked to pay in cash or Venmo. Paying outside WrenchNow loses your protections, and it breaks the mechanic's agreement. Use "Asked to pay outside WrenchNow?" on the booking and we'll follow up.

A refund. Refunds are decided after hearing from both sides, and go back to your card through Stripe.

Your car's history, kept for you

Every booking adds to My car: what was done, when, and at what mileage. WrenchNow tracks common service intervals and can email you when something is due.

Mechanics can attach an inspection report that marks each item good, due soon or needs attention now. Anything flagged shows up on your list, ready to book.

For mechanics

One-time setup, then quote the jobs you want.

  1. Apply and get checked

    Apply with your experience and the work you do. You'll get a background and driving-record check invite by email. Full-service mechanics also share ASE certification or two references.

  2. Set up payouts

    Connect your bank through Stripe once. Then set your rate, travel distance and whether you offer free diagnosis.

  3. Quote, work, get paid

    Quote requests near you show up in My jobs. When a customer accepts, you get their address and phone. Payment goes to your bank after the job.
