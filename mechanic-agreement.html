Mechanic Independent Contractor Agreement

Last updated: October 12, 2026. WrenchNow, Denver, Colorado. Questions: hello@getwrenchnow.com

By submitting an application to join WrenchNow and by performing any services through the platform, you ("Mechanic") agree to this Independent Contractor Agreement with WrenchNow ("WrenchNow").

1. Independent Contractor Status

You are an independent contractor, not an employee of WrenchNow. This Agreement does not create an employment relationship, agency, partnership, or joint venture. You have no authority to bind WrenchNow to any contract or obligation.

As an independent contractor, you are solely responsible for:

  • All taxes arising from payments received through WrenchNow, including self-employment tax
  • Obtaining and maintaining all required licences, certifications, and permits to perform automotive services in your jurisdiction
  • Providing your own tools, equipment, and supplies
  • Setting your own working hours and schedule
  • Determining how services are performed, subject to customer requirements
  • Complying with all applicable laws and regulations

2. Qualifications and Representations

By applying to WrenchNow, you represent and warrant that:

  • You are at least 18 years of age
  • You have the legal right to work in the jurisdiction where you will provide services
  • All information you provide in your application is accurate and complete
  • You hold all licences, certifications, and qualifications required by applicable law to perform the services you offer
  • You have not been convicted of a crime that would reasonably disqualify you from working with members of the public
  • You are in good health and physically capable of performing automotive services safely
  • You will maintain the accuracy of your profile information at all times

3. Insurance Requirements

You are required to maintain, at your own expense, adequate insurance coverage to protect yourself and your customers, including:

  • General liability insurance (recommended minimum: $1,000,000 per occurrence)
  • Garage keeper's liability or on-hook towing insurance if applicable
  • Workers' compensation insurance if required by your jurisdiction
  • Any other insurance required by applicable law

WrenchNow does not provide insurance coverage for Mechanics or their work. WrenchNow is not responsible for any claims, losses, or damages arising from your failure to maintain adequate insurance.

4. Quality of Work

You agree to:

  • Perform all services in a professional, workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards
  • Use appropriate parts and materials for each job
  • Communicate clearly with customers about the scope, cost, and timeline of work before beginning
  • Obtain customer approval before performing any work beyond the original agreed scope
  • Leave the customer's vehicle and property in the same or better condition than found
  • Respond promptly to booking notifications and customer communications
  • Arrive within the agreed time window or notify the customer immediately of any delay

5. Prohibited Conduct

You agree not to:

  • Perform services while impaired by alcohol, drugs, or any substance
  • Misrepresent your qualifications, experience, or the services you provide
  • Charge amounts beyond those agreed with the customer and disclosed on the platform
  • Take payment outside WrenchNow for a job found on WrenchNow, including cash, Venmo, Zelle or similar
  • Harass, intimidate, or behave inappropriately toward any customer
  • Share customer personal information with any third party
  • Use WrenchNow for any unlawful purpose
  • Damage a customer's vehicle, property, or any third-party property intentionally or through gross negligence
  • Solicit customers to use your services outside the WrenchNow platform for 12 months after your last booking through WrenchNow
  • Miss confirmed appointments without notice. Missed appointments and cancellations within 24 hours of a job are shown on your profile and lower your place in search; repeated no-shows can lead to removal

6. Platform Fee and Payments

Platform fee. WrenchNow's platform fee is 5% of the job price on every job paid through WrenchNow.

Background check: onboarding fee. WrenchNow orders a third-party background and driving-record check for each applicant. When joining is free (no upfront verification fee), you agree that WrenchNow recovers the cost of your check through an onboarding fee of $50 in total, deducted from your first paid jobs at no more than $10 per job and no more than 15% of any single job, until $50 has been collected. The onboarding fee is only taken from jobs you are paid for; if a job is fully refunded, the amount taken from it is credited back toward your remaining balance. If you never complete a paid job, you owe nothing. Each deduction is shown with the job. When an upfront verification fee of $49 is charged instead, no onboarding fee applies, and the verification fee is not refunded once the check has been ordered.

You will get at least 30 days' notice by email before the platform fee changes, and a change never applies to jobs already accepted.

Quotes and price changes. You set your price for each job as a quote (labor and parts). If you find more work is needed, send a revised price through WrenchNow before doing the extra work; the customer must approve it, and if they don't, the original price stands.

Getting paid. All jobs are paid through WrenchNow. You must complete Stripe payout setup (Stripe Connect) before sending quotes. The customer's payment goes to your Stripe account, less card processing costs (about 3.2% + 55¢ per job, covering Stripe's card and payout fees), the 5% platform fee, and any onboarding fee still owed. Payment is taken when the customer confirms the job, or 48 hours after you mark it done unless the customer reports a problem first. If a customer reports a problem or disputes the charge with their bank, payment may be held, and if a refund is issued, the amount paid to you for that job may be reversed.

Payments are processed through Stripe Connect. WrenchNow is not responsible for delays or failures in payment processing caused by Stripe or by errors in your account information.

7. Liability and Indemnification

You are solely responsible for any damage, injury, loss, or claim arising from the services you perform through WrenchNow. WrenchNow assumes no liability for your work, your vehicle, your tools, or your conduct.

You agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless WrenchNow and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising out of or relating to:

  • Any service you perform or fail to perform
  • Any damage to a customer's vehicle or property
  • Any personal injury caused by you or arising from your work
  • Your violation of this Agreement or any applicable law
  • Your misrepresentation of your qualifications or the services you provide

8. Ratings and Reviews

Customers may rate and review your services after each completed job. WrenchNow may display these ratings publicly on your profile. WrenchNow reserves the right to remove any review that violates its policies. You may not solicit, incentivise, or manipulate reviews in any way.

WrenchNow may suspend or remove Mechanics whose average rating falls below acceptable thresholds as determined by WrenchNow in its sole discretion.

9. Termination

WrenchNow may suspend or terminate your access to the platform at any time, for any reason, including but not limited to:

  • Violation of this Agreement or WrenchNow's Terms of Service
  • Customer complaints or consistently low ratings
  • Fraudulent or illegal conduct
  • Failure to maintain required licences or insurance
  • Any conduct that WrenchNow determines, in its sole discretion, is harmful to customers, the platform, or WrenchNow's reputation

You may terminate this Agreement at any time by ceasing to use the platform.

10. Confidentiality

You agree to keep confidential all customer personal information accessed through the platform, including names, addresses, vehicle information, and contact details. You may not use customer information for any purpose other than performing the booked service.

11. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute between you and WrenchNow arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration on an individual basis. You waive any right to participate in class action proceedings.

12. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by applicable law in the jurisdiction where WrenchNow is incorporated.

13. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with WrenchNow's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, constitutes the entire agreement between you and WrenchNow regarding your participation as a Mechanic on the platform.

14. Contact

For questions about this Agreement, contact legal@wrenchnow.com.
