A fairer way to get your car fixed

WrenchNow is a Denver company connecting drivers with independent mobile mechanics.

Why we built it

Getting a car fixed usually means a morning in a waiting room, a price you can't compare, and a shop markup on every hour and every part. Meanwhile, skilled mechanics who work for themselves spend their time chasing customers and payments.

WrenchNow puts the two together. Drivers get upfront quotes from mechanics who come to them. Mechanics set their own prices, keep most of every job, and get paid through the app.

What we stand behind

  • Prices agreed before work starts. Changes need the customer's approval in the app.
  • Checked mechanics. Every mechanic passes a background and driving-record check before they can take jobs.
  • Honest reviews. Only paid jobs booked here can be reviewed. We don't buy or filter reviews.
  • Plain fees. 5% from mechanics per job. Every fee is written down on the pricing page and in our agreements.

Where we are

We're launching in the Denver area and adding cities as mechanics join. If you'd like WrenchNow where you live, email us your ZIP code.
