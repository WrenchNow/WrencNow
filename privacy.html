Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026 · WrenchNow, Denver, Colorado · hello@getwrenchnow.com
WrenchNow is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Name, email address, and password when you create an account
  • Phone number when booking a service, shared with the mechanic you book so they can reach you
  • Vehicle information (year, make, model) when booking a service
  • Location or address when booking a service
  • Payment information (processed and stored by Stripe — we never see or store your card number)
  • Service descriptions and notes you provide when booking
  • Reviews and ratings you submit
  • Information you provide when applying as a Mechanic, including contact details, professional experience, qualifications, and an optional profile photo
  • For Mechanic applicants: the result of a background and driving-record check run by a third-party provider with your consent (we receive the result, not your Social Security number)

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address and general location data
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and features used on the platform
  • Booking and transaction history

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the WrenchNow platform
  • To connect you with Mechanics or Customers
  • To process payments and send booking confirmations
  • To send service reminders and re-engagement communications
  • To verify the identity and credentials of Mechanics
  • To investigate disputes, fraud, or misuse of the platform
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To communicate platform updates, new features, and promotional offers (you may opt out at any time)

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With Mechanics: When you ask for quotes, mechanics see your first name, vehicle, the job, any photos you add, and your ZIP code / rough area. Your exact address and phone number are shared only with the one Mechanic whose quote you accept
  • With Customers: Mechanic profiles including name, location, specialties, ratings, and verified job prices are visible to all users
  • With service providers: We use Google Firebase (database and sign-in), Stripe (payments), Resend (email), and, for mechanic applicants who consent, a background-check provider to operate the platform. These providers process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations
  • For legal reasons: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of WrenchNow, its users, or the public
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction

4. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Booking records are retained for a minimum of 7 years for legal and tax compliance purposes. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time (subject to legal retention requirements) by emailing privacy@wrenchnow.com.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), access rules on our database that limit who can read each record, and secure third-party payment processing through Stripe. We never store payment card numbers. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and Tracking

WrenchNow uses your browser's local storage (not advertising cookies) to keep you signed in until you sign out, and session storage to speed up page loads. We do not use persistent tracking cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data for marketing purposes
  • Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw consent for data processing where consent is the legal basis

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@wrenchnow.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

WrenchNow is not intended for use by persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored in countries other than your own. By using WrenchNow, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries that may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

10. Third-Party Links

The platform may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

11. GDPR — Users in the European Economic Area

If you are located in the EEA, our legal basis for processing your personal data is: (a) performance of a contract when we process data to provide our services; (b) legitimate interests when we improve the platform and prevent fraud; (c) compliance with legal obligations; and (d) consent where you have provided it. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

12. CCPA — Users in California

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion of personal information, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. WrenchNow does not sell personal information. To exercise your rights under the CCPA, contact us at privacy@wrenchnow.com.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

14. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at privacy@wrenchnow.com.
