Payment. All jobs booked on WrenchNow are paid through WrenchNow. The Customer's card is saved through Stripe, Inc. when they accept a quote and is charged the agreed (or approved revised) price when the Customer confirms the job is done, or 48 hours after the Mechanic marks it done unless the Customer reports a problem first.
