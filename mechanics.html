Two ways to join

Basic maintenance

Oil changes, batteries, tires and rotation, filters, bulbs, wipers, fluid top-offs and jump starts. No certification needed.

Full-service repair

Brakes, diagnostics, starters and alternators, cooling, suspension and more. Needs ASE certification or three years of experience with references.

What you need

  • Your own tools and transportation
  • A clean background and driving-record check, run by our screening partner
  • A bank account for Stripe payouts
  • Liability insurance is optional, and earns an Insured badge that ranks you higher

How you rank

Best match weighs your reviews most, then showing up for booked jobs, jobs completed, verification and insurance. Only jobs paid through WrenchNow count. Missed appointments and cancellations within 24 hours are shown on your profile.
