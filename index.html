QUESTIONS WE GET A LOT

We know car repairs feel risky. Here's the honest truth.

"How do I know the price is fair?"
Every mechanic's rate is posted upfront. Before work starts, you get a written estimate. You approve it or cancel — no charge.
"Are these mechanics actually any good?"
Look for the Verified badge. It means we ran a background and driving-record check, confirmed their ASE certification or called their references, and have their liability insurance on file. Star ratings only appear once a mechanic has completed jobs here.
"What if I have no idea what's wrong with my car?"
Most customers don't — and that's exactly why we built this. Just describe what you're experiencing ("weird noise," "won't start," "yellow light on"). Your mechanic diagnoses it on-site and walks you through everything in plain English before touching a thing.
"What if the problem comes back?"
If the same issue returns after your service, contact the mechanic directly first — most will come back and make it right. If that doesn't settle it, tell us and we'll help mediate between you.
