QUESTIONS WE GET A LOT

We know car repairs feel risky. Here's the honest truth.

"How do I know the price is fair?"
Every mechanic's rate is posted upfront. Before work starts, you get a written estimate. You approve it or cancel — no charge. We also show real prices from completed jobs so you know what others actually paid.
"Are these mechanics actually any good?"
Every mechanic passes a background check, license verification, and skills review before joining. Reviews come only from verified completed jobs — no way to fake them. Average rating across the platform: 4.9 stars.
"What if I have no idea what's wrong with my car?"
Most customers don't — and that's exactly why we built this. Just describe what you're experiencing ("weird noise," "won't start," "yellow light on"). Your mechanic diagnoses it on-site and walks you through everything in plain English before touching a thing.
"What if the problem comes back?"
If the same issue returns after your service, contact the mechanic directly — they're a vetted professional who stands behind their work. WrenchNow also has a dispute process to help mediate if needed. We vet every mechanic carefully so you're in good hands.
