Common questions

What is a mobile mechanic?

A mobile mechanic is a licensed or experienced auto technician who drives to you, with their tools and parts, and repairs your car where it is parked: your driveway, street, office lot or apartment garage, where allowed. Most maintenance and many repairs can be done this way.

Does it cost anything to ask for quotes?

No. Requesting and comparing quotes is free. You pay the price you accept, and nothing more unless you approve a change.

When is my card charged?

Your card is saved securely by Stripe when you accept a quote. It's charged the final approved price after the job: when you press Confirm and pay, or 48 hours after your mechanic marks the job done. Report a problem before then and the payment is held.

What if the mechanic finds more work?

They send a revised price through the app with the reason. You approve or reject it on your phone. If you reject it, the original price stands.

Who sees my address?

Mechanics who quote see your ZIP code and rough area only. Your street address and phone go to the one mechanic whose quote you accept.

How are mechanics checked?

Every mechanic passes a third-party background and driving-record check before their profile goes live. Full-service mechanics also show ASE certification or references. Mechanics with verified liability insurance carry an Insured badge. Checks lower the risk but can't guarantee future conduct; the Terms of Service explain the details.

What if the mechanic doesn't show up?

Report it from My bookings after the appointment time. You aren't charged, and missed appointments appear on the mechanic's profile and lower their ranking.

Is a mobile mechanic cheaper than a shop or dealer?

Often, because a mobile mechanic has no shop rent and no service-desk markup. Every quote on WrenchNow shows the mechanic's price next to an estimate of what a dealer would charge, so you can see the difference before you book.

What can't a mobile mechanic do?

Jobs that need a vehicle lift or heavy shop equipment, such as transmission rebuilds, major engine work, wheel alignment or mounting new tires, usually need a shop. If your job needs one, the mechanic will tell you before quoting.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free, any time before work starts, and your card isn't charged. If you booked a paid diagnosis and the mechanic has already done it, the diagnosis price is still due.

Who pays for parts?

Parts are included in the quote unless it says otherwise, and the mechanic brings them. If you'd rather supply your own, say so in your request. You can also ask to keep the old parts that are replaced.

Is the work guaranteed?

Each mechanic sets their own warranty on parts and labor, shown on their profile and your receipt. If something goes wrong, report it in the app before you're charged and the payment is held while we sort it out.

Where is WrenchNow available?

We're launching in the Denver area and adding cities as mechanics join. Each mechanic sets how far they travel, and you'll only see mechanics who cover your address. If none do yet, send your request anyway and we'll try to find you one.
